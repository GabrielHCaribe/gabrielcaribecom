Learning robotics through designing, programming, and building a drone from scratch.

Project Goals

I have the following 2 goals:

  • Build a basic Wi-Fi controlled drone that can fly and balance as usual
  • Integrate Computer Vision to get to get the drone to follow me around autonomously

Additionally, I am on a student budget, so I will keep this project as cheap as possible (~50 Eruos).

Current Progress

All components are selected. I will be using the following:

  • Motors, Propellers, and Motor Control (36.99 Euros)
    • A2212 1000KV motors
    • 1045 Propellers
    • 30A Electronic Speed Controllers
  • Battery and Charger (17.38 Euros)
    • 3S 11.1V 3500mAh LiPo Battery
    • LiPo Battery Charger
  • MPU6500 6-Axis Gyroscopic Sensor (1.89 Euros)
  • ESP-WROOM-32 (Free - already owned)
  • 5V 3A UBEC Voltage Regulator (2.59 Euros)
  • Custom Frame (Free - 3D printed)

The total cost of the components is 58.85 Euros - slightly overbudget, but these are the cheapest components I could find. Unfortunetly, after shipping and customs fees were ~30 Euros so I ended up being very overbudget :(

With the selected motors and propellers, the drone should be able to carry at least 1.5kg of extra weight - more than enough for the future upgrades!

The CAD model is in progress and I am working on the code in parallel.

More updates coming soon!
